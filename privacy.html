Privacy Policy

Rung is offered by Abhinav Chauhan. This policy covers the English iPhone app version 1.0, build 93, and these support pages. For privacy questions or requests, email support@nudgeclean.app.

Practice data on your iPhone

Rung stores your ladder, ratings, notes, scripts, recordings, photos, logs, check-ins, and saved links on your iPhone. Rung does not require an account or sync that practice data to a Rung server. Limited timer and count information can be shared with Rung’s widget and Live Activity on the same device. The app marks its private storage as excluded from Apple device backups; you can make an encrypted Rung backup yourself in Settings.

Your practice data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Apple’s operating system may handle device functions such as notifications and local storage under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store payments. Rung uses RevenueCat to present plans, check Pro access, restore purchases, and analyze subscription activity. RevenueCat receives purchase history, subscription status, and a randomly generated anonymous app user ID. Rung does not give RevenueCat your practice text, ratings, photos, or recordings, and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies, including Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Exports, links, and support messages

When you export written data or an encrypted backup, the iPhone’s share or save control sends the file only to the destination you choose. Written exports are not encrypted. Rung cannot delete copies you previously saved or shared. Opening a saved web link lets that site process your visit under its own policy.

If you email support, we receive your address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive practice details unless necessary. We keep support messages as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations, then delete them when no longer needed.

Analytics, diagnostics, and this website

Version 1.0 build 93 is not configured to send usage analytics to PostHog or crash reports to Sentry. Those services are inactive in this build. If that changes in a later build, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before enabling collection.

These pages do not include our own analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies. They are served by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as your IP address under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links and your email provider have their own practices.

Deletion, retention, and choices

Use Rung Settings to delete on-device practice data. This removes local practice records and media, local notifications, and widget snapshots. You may also remove the app from your iPhone. Neither action cancels an Apple subscription, deletes exported copies, or erases purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. You can manage subscriptions with Apple.

To ask about support-message data or purchase records we can identify, email support@nudgeclean.app. We may need enough information to verify the request; please do not send passwords or card numbers. Apple and RevenueCat may need to handle records they control directly, and some records may need to be retained under applicable law.

Security, age, and changes

Rung uses iOS storage protections for local data. Encrypted Rung backups require a passphrase you choose; keep it safe because we cannot recover it. No storage or transmission method is risk free. Rung is not directed to children below the App Store age rating.

We may update this policy when the app or service changes. The effective date above will change, and we will give additional notice where required.
