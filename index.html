Help and contact

Rung Support

Rung helps you make your own practice ladder, time a session, and reflect on what you tried. It is a self-help practice tool. Not a substitute for professional care.

Contact

For app problems, purchases, or privacy requests, email support@nudgeclean.app. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not include private practice notes, recordings, passwords, or payment-card details in your first message.

Purchases and subscriptions

If Pro is missing, open Rung and tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings. Check that you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still does not work, email us with the plan and approximate purchase date; an Apple order ID may help, but never send payment-card details.

Apple handles billing. You can manage or cancel a subscription with your Apple Account and request a refund from Apple. Deleting Rung does not cancel a subscription. Prices, trial eligibility, and renewal terms are shown before purchase.

Your data

Practice entries are stored on your iPhone. Settings lets you export written data, make an encrypted backup, or delete on-device Rung data. A written export is not encrypted. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription or remove copies you previously exported. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Need immediate help?

Rung is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger or might hurt yourself, call your local emergency number now. Rung’s Help now screen lists support lines for several countries.
